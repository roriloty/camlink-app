Privacy policy

CamLink USB and CamLink Lite (Android). Last updated 13 November 2026 (version 1.11).

Summary

CamLink runs entirely on your phone. There is no CamLink server and no account. The developer does not receive any of your photos, files or personal information. The apps contain no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What the apps handle

  • Photos from your camera. They are saved to your phone (Pictures/CamLink). If you turn on auto-edit, the edited copy is made on the phone.
  • Upload settings. Your FTP server address, user name and password, and the Google account and Drive folder you choose. These are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Upload history. A list of uploaded photos with small previews, stored only on your phone so you can review or delete them.

Google user data

If you turn on Google Drive upload and sign in, the app asks for the limited Google Drive scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). With it, CamLink can only see and change files and folders it created itself. It cannot see any of your other Drive files. The app uses it only to:

  • create the upload folder you name (for example “CamLink”) and its “Edited” and “Original” subfolders;
  • list the folders it created, so you can choose one again later (including after you move it into a shared drive);
  • upload your photos into that folder;
  • move a photo the app uploaded to the Drive trash when you delete it in the app's upload gallery.

The app also reads your account's email address to show which account is signed in. Data goes directly between your phone and Google's servers. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train any AI or machine-learning model. Sign-in is handled by Google Play services; the app does not see or store your Google password.

CamLink's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

FTP

If you set up FTP upload, photos are sent to the server you enter and nowhere else. Turn on FTPS in the settings to encrypt the connection.

Your control

  • Turn uploads off or sign out of Google at any time in the app's Upload settings. Signing out also revokes the app's access to your Drive. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete any photo from the upload gallery. This removes it from the phone, the FTP server, and moves it to the Drive trash.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all its settings and history from your phone. Photos saved in Pictures/CamLink stay until you delete them.

Children

CamLink is a general photography tool and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. For questions, open an issue at github.com/roriloty/camlink-app/issues.
