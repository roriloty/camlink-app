CamLink

Tether a Sony Alpha camera to your Android phone with a USB cable. Photos are saved in full quality, can be auto-edited, and can be uploaded to your FTP server or Google Drive.

Download Source code

Camera control

Live view, tap to focus, aperture, shutter, ISO, EV, white balance and a remote shutter.

Upload only

No live view, so it saves power. It receives photos in the background, even with the phone locked.

Auto-edit

An on-device edit that keeps highlights and contrast, sets whites and blacks, and leaves night scenes dark.

Upload

To FTP/FTPS and/or a CamLink folder in Google Drive. There's a gallery where you can delete photos you don't want.

Google Drive

If you turn on Google Drive upload, CamLink asks for limited access to your Google Drive: it can only see files and folders it created itself. It creates an upload folder (and Original/Edited subfolders), uploads your photos there, and moves a photo to the Drive trash when you delete it in the app. You can move that folder into a shared drive or share it, and CamLink keeps uploading to it. There is no CamLink server: your photos go straight from your phone to your Drive. See the privacy policy.

Supported cameras

Developed and tested with the Sony A7 III. Other Sony Alpha bodies that support “PC Remote” over USB may work.
